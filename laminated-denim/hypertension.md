The second song on *[Laminated Denim](https://kglw.net/discography/laminated-denim)* is “Hypertension,” a song seemingly about someone time traveling and experiencing hypertension (high blood pressure) while doing so. It features a number of references to the band’s other work: notes outside the scale (microtones), harbinger of changes (the album *[Changes](https://kglw.net/discography/changes)* which was released shortly after *Laminated Denim)*, and sticky red death ([“Lava”](https://kglw.net/song/lava) from *[Ice, Death…](https://kglw.net/discography/ice-death-planets-lungs-mushrooms-and-lava)*). The song also features a gong hit every five minutes and a polyrhythm accenting at 60 bpm, reinforcing its ties to the passing of time found in the *[Made In Timeland](https://kglw.net/discography/made-in-timeland)* concept. Taking influence from progressive and classic rock, “Hypertension” is a sprawling track with multiple instrumental sections and solos that build to a climactic end. The song’s main riff is also interpolated towards the end of [“The Land Before Timeland.”](https://kglw.net/song/the-land-before-timeland)

According to liner notes, the song was performed by Ambrose (keyboards/percussion/vocals), Cavs (drums/percussion), Cook (guitar), Joey (guitar/bass/synthesizer) and Stu (vocals/guitar/bass/keys/percussion/flute). Before the song was even released it was being hyped up. In Cavs’ [drum seminar at Drumtek](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M9O9b-U0nl4&t=2822s) in Northcote, he described it as a fifteen-minute “space rock odyssey” and mentioned that it was one of his all time favorite Gizzard songs. Before the album’s release, a countdown clock with the song was played during the band’s intermission on [2022-10-11](https://kglw.net/setlists/king-gizzard-the-lizard-wizard-october-11-2022-red-rocks-amphitheatre-morrison-co-usa.html). “Hypertension” was later released on October 12th 2022. Alongside the album release, the band uploaded the [official countdown clock](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BDJ3nQAanMA) visualiser made by Jason Galea.

The song had been teased as far back as [2022-08-03](https://kglw.net/setlists/king-gizzard-the-lizard-wizard-august-3-2022-st-michaels-fortress-sibenik-croatia.html) where Stu sang a verse during [“Am I In Heaven?”](https://kglw.net/song/am-i-in-heaven). “Hypertension” saw a live debut on [2022-10-23](https://kglw.net/setlists/king-gizzard-the-lizard-wizard-october-23-2022-the-anthem-washington-dc-usa.html) at The Anthem in Washington D.C., appearing as the opener. In the following shows, however, the song would become a destination rather than a starting point coming out of songs like [“Hot Water”](https://kglw.net/song/hot-water) ([2022-10-26](https://kglw.net/setlists/king-gizzard-the-lizard-wizard-october-26-2022-the-eastern-atlanta-ga-usa.html)), and [“Iron Lung”](https://kglw.net/song/iron-lung) ([2022-10-27](https://kglw.net/setlists/king-gizzard-the-lizard-wizard-october-27-2022-orpheum-theater-new-orleans-la-usa.html)). The song would be played heavily throughout 2023. At the beginning of the year it was back to being a standalone song but began to break back out of that role at the beginning of the residency tour on [2023-06-01](https://kglw.net/setlists/king-gizzard-the-lizard-wizard-june-1-2023-the-caverns-pelham-tn-usa.html) where it often came out of [“Ice V”](https://kglw.net/song/ice-v) or “Iron Lung.” Typically, live performances of the song are shorter than the studio version.
