Opening *[Laminated Denim](https://kglw.net/discography/laminated-denim)* is a bright and breezy fifteen-minute prog rock/Krautrock jam called “The Land Before Timeland.” The lyrics are strange, focusing on a process of self-actualization with the help of demons and potentially even time travel. It also namedrops [“The River”](https://kglw.net/song/the-river) and [“Rattlesnake”](https://kglw.net/song/rattlesnake), and interpolates the main riff of [“Hypertension”](https://kglw.net/song/hypertension), connecting it to the Gizzverse at large. The song features a gong hit every five minutes as well as a accenting rhythm at 60 bpm, tying the song back to *[Made In Timeland](https://kglw.net/discography/made-in-timeland)*’s concept.

“The Land Before Timeland” was played by Ambrose (keyboards/percussion/harmonica/vocals), Cavs (drums/percussion), Cook (bass/guitar), Joey (guitar/bass/synthesizer), Lucas (keyboards) and Stu (vocals/guitars/keyboards/synthesizer/percussion). It was first heard during the band’s intermission on [2022-10-10](https://kglw.net/setlists/king-gizzard-the-lizard-wizard-october-10-2022-red-rocks-amphitheatre-morrison-co-usa.html). *Laminated Denim* was released on October 12th, 2022. Alongside the album’s release, the band uploaded the [official countdown clock](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yTm7wzsoU6I) visualiser made by Jason Galea. The countdown clock for “The Land Before Timeland” features a wire frame version of the snake statue seen on the cover of *[Chunky Shrapnel](https://kglw.net/discography/chunky-shrapnel)* at 4:34.

The song has never been played live, but was teased as far back as [2022-10-24](https://kglw.net/setlists/king-gizzard-the-lizard-wizard-october-24-2022-rabbit-rabbit-asheville-nc-usa.html). That said, the band has been meaning to play it live. In the live chat before the debut of the [music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T4bME3bbO1Q) for the GUM/Ambrose track “Ill Times,” Ambrose said “Gizz has been meaning to practice land before tideland [sic]. Haven’t gotten around to it yet but its on the cards!”
